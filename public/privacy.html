Privacy Policy — Playlist Picker

Last updated: 2026-08-26

Playlist Picker is a small personal app that lets a host's friends add songs to a shared YouTube Music playlist. This page explains what data the app collects and how it's used.

What we collect

When a friend joins, they provide a name and phone number. The phone number is used only to identify repeat visits and enforce a 2-song limit per person — it is not verified, not used for messaging, and not shared with any third party.

When a friend selects songs, the chosen YouTube video IDs, titles, artists, and thumbnails are stored so we can show a confirmation and prevent duplicate submissions.

How we use it

This data is used solely to operate the app: tracking who has already used their picks, and adding selected songs to the host's YouTube playlist via the YouTube Data API. We do not sell, rent, or share this data, and we do not use it for advertising.

YouTube API Services

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using this app, you also agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and Google's Privacy Policy. The app only requests access needed to search for videos and add them to the host's own playlist — it does not access the host's other Google or YouTube data.

Data storage and retention

Data is stored in a database controlled by the app's host. The host can delete this data at any time by removing the relevant records.

Contact

Questions about this app or your data can be sent to the host directly.
